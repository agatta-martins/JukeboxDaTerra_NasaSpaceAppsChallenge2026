Pick an image of Earth and hear what it shows

Each image is read from left to right. Color, brightness and texture become notes on a musical scale.

The image pixels could not be read to play the preview. Open the site through a local server (for example, python -m http.server inside the project folder) instead of double-clicking the file.

How an image becomes sound

The image is split into 48 columns. Each column plays one note, and the same list of notes drives the audio, the golden line over the image and the piano roll. The notes are locked to a scale so they sound like music rather than noise.

In the imageIn the sound
Horizontal positionTiming of the music and stereo position, from left to right
Brightest region of the columnPitch: the higher it sits in the image, the higher the note
BrightnessNote volume
Warm tonesBright timbre, with the filter open
Cool tonesSoft, rounded timbre
GreenShort, marimba-like timbre
Texture and detailA second voice joins where the image has more contrast
Average image brightnessStrength of a low background chord
